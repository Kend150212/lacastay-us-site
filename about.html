Our story

LacaStay started with a simple observation: the phone rings when you're busiest. Every missed call is a customer who found someone else.

We built an AI receptionist that answers in under a second, books into your real calendar, and never has an off day — at a price a single-location business can actually afford.

Today LacaStay serves nail salons, spas, and vacation rentals around the world. Our mission is global: every local business — in any country, in any language — deserves a front desk that never misses.
